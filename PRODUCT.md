# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro 5, TypeScript, static output to `dist`, based on the supplied Antons local-site code and prepared for the production domain `https://blockeddrainshillsdistrict.com.au`.

## Users

Homeowners, landlords, strata contacts, property managers and local businesses across Sydney's Hills District who are dealing with a slow, blocked, overflowing or repeatedly failing drain, usually while searching on a phone and needing a clear next step.

## Product Purpose

Generate qualified phone enquiries for professional blocked-drain diagnosis, clearing and repair while giving customers genuinely useful information about causes, safe immediate actions, professional methods, equipment, repair options and local property considerations.

## Positioning

Antons Blocked Drains Hills District is a specialist local drain-service brand backed by the established Antons team. It distinguishes professional cause-led diagnosis and durable repair planning from temporary DIY clearing.

## Operating Context

Visitors may be facing a blocked toilet, slow sink or shower, gurgling pipework, sewage overflow, stormwater backup, tree-root intrusion or a recurring blockage. They need to know what is urgent, what to stop doing, how professional drain clearing works and whether the service covers their Hills District address.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public brand: Antons Blocked Drains Hills District.
- Production domain: `https://blockeddrainshillsdistrict.com.au` using the non-`www` hostname and trailing slashes.
- Primary conversion: phone call to 0493 824 176 (`tel:+61493824176`).
- Urgent calls are taken 24 hours, 7 days a week; no fixed arrival-time promise.
- Core services include blocked toilets, blocked sinks and floor wastes, sewer and stormwater drains, high-pressure water jetting, CCTV drain inspection, tree-root diagnosis, drain excavation and repair, and recurring-blockage prevention.
- Local pages must be useful and materially distinct, not suburb-name swaps.
- Service coverage is confirmed by full address when the caller books.
- Static, responsive, accessible and fast, with minimal JavaScript and no unnecessary third-party requests.
- Do not invent prices, guarantees, job locations, testimonials, awards, response times or diagnostic certainty.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the customer's symptom and a safe, practical next step.
- Diagnose the cause before recommending the clearing or repair method.
- Show professional tools, equipment and real work as evidence of capability.
- Explain temporary relief versus lasting repair honestly.
- Build substantial local pages around property types, access, drainage conditions and neighbouring coverage.
- Make licensing, operator responsibility and phone access easy to verify without overwhelming the service copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA, support keyboard navigation and reduced motion, use visible focus states, maintain readable contrast and provide large mobile call targets.
